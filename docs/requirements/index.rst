Requirements
============

Two kinds of requirement, one record format (see :doc:`/reading-guide`):

* :doc:`bsi_tr_03183_2_v1_1` — taken from the BSI guideline, grouped by the
  section it comes from (``origin: standard``).
* :doc:`product` — product decisions such as exit codes and reports
  (``origin: product``).
* :doc:`register` — all of them in one searchable, sortable table.
